Most levels require you to collect all the Brains to pass them.
The "GOAL" meter at the upper-right corner indicates how many
Brains are left. Whenever it's missing, that means you can go
complete the level - usually by running into a hub exit.

Another thing: keep a watchful eye for any Candles lurking about.
They're optional towards level completion, but required if you
care about getting a 100% completion rate. The designers usually
hide them behind walls and obstacles, though sometimes they get
rather creative! Yerf!

... Oh, and steer clear of any mummy-adjacent creatures you may
happen to come across. They do not want to give you a hug.
